The app's parts

Outside a session there is time: setup, preparation, the account. Same tokens, roomier sizes. Every control shows its state with fill, edge and weight; the pointer stays an arrow, and fields show a text caret instead of an I-beam.

A

Buttons

One primary action per view. Destructive only outside a session.

The arrow-out icon means the button opens your browser: sign-in, checkout and billing never happen inside the app.

B

Toggles

A switch for an on/off setting, a segmented control for two to five choices, a checkbox for agreeing.

Ticked before every session, never remembered. Final words wait for legal review.

C

A list instead of a dropdown

A native dropdown opens its own window. Choices longer than a segmented control are an in-window list, or a panel that expands in place.
