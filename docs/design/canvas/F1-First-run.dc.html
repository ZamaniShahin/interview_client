Before your first session: what goes where

A draft for layout; the final words wait for legal review.

On this computer

Your history, documents and prepared answers, encrypted. Transcripts and recordings only if you turn them on.

To Sotto's server

The interviewer's voice (never your microphone), your documents' text and what you type. Used for the session, never stored.

To OpenAI, which writes the answers and transcribes the interviewer

Your documents, what the interviewer says, the questions and the answers, with nothing that identifies you. OpenAI may keep them for up to 30 days to watch for abuse, then deletes them.

Test hiding

Sotto's windows are left out of screen shares and recordings. Check it once with a real share.

  1. Start sharing your whole screen in your call app: Zoom, Meet or Teams. A meeting with only yourself is enough.
  2. Press Show the overlay. It appears on your screen with a test card.
  3. Look at what the share sends, in its preview or from a second device. The overlay and this window should not be in it.

"I can see it" opens the fixes: a call app's own capture mode, or an older Windows version. Finishing doesn't need the test.
